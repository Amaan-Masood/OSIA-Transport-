## ADR #02 — Multi-page static site instead of a single page
ADR #: 02
Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-11-11

Context:
- Earlier brief required a single-page scrolling site. Target searches are service-specific ("mobile crane rental Abu Dhabi", "sweet water supply Abu Dhabi", "septic tank cleaning Abu Dhabi"). Local competitors that rank use separate URLs per service.

Decision:
- One URL per service plus services hub, about, FAQ, contact, legal pages, and area pages only where the owner confirms the area and real unique content exists. The home page still contains the full one-page story (all earlier sections), so the "single page" feel is kept.

Alternatives:
- Option A — single page with anchors: simplest; one title/description/H1 for seven topics; cannot rank per service.
- Option B — many near-duplicate area pages: rejected (thin/doorway content risk).

Consequences:
- Positive: per-service titles, schema and internal linking; better conversion (ad landing pages); shareable URLs.
- Negative: more copy to write and keep accurate; more tests; sitemap/canonical care.
- Follow-ups: per-tonnage crane pages and more areas in Phase 2 after owner supplies real details.
